<Update label="August 31, 2026" description="Release 2026.08.2" tags={["Platform", "Video Agents", "Chatbots"]} rss={{ title: "Company roles and permissions, video agent dashboards, chatbot skills" }}>

## Company roles and permissions

You can now define roles for your company and decide what each one can do. Menu items, and the create, edit, and delete actions in every module (Apps included), follow the signed-in user's role. Users only see the parts of UnleashX they're meant to use, which makes it safer to give access to larger teams.

## Video agent dashboards

Video Agents now have their own dashboards and statistics, so you can track video conversations the same way you track voice calls.

<Info>
  **Renamed:** the *Realtime* engine mode for voice agents is now called **Speech-to-Speech (S2S)**.

  **Moved:** Forms is now a tab inside **Apps** and no longer has its own sidebar item. Existing forms are unchanged.
</Info>

### Chatbots

**Skills**: Chatbots now support skills: reusable capabilities you add to a chatbot to extend what it can do.

### Campaigns and Audience

**Activity logs**: Campaigns and audiences now keep an activity log, so you can see who changed what and when.

</Update>

<Update label="August 15, 2026" description="Release 2026.08.1" tags={["Video Agents", "Voice AI", "Billing"]} rss={{ title: "Introducing Video Agents, payment-failure alerts" }}>

## Introducing Video Agents

**Video Agents** is a new module for building AI agents that hold face-to-face conversations. Configure a video agent's behaviour like a voice agent then share it with anyone through a direct link.

## Payment failure alerts

If a payment fails, a banner appears across the app saying *"Your last payment attempt failed. Please update your payment method to avoid service interruption."* It links straight to retrying the payment, so billing problems get noticed before they affect your agents.

<Info>
  **Renamed:** *Structured Outputs* is now **Analysis**, and audience groups are now called **Segments**.
</Info>

### Leasha

**Media in Leasha**: Leasha, the in-app assistant, now accepts images and files as part of your question.

</Update>
