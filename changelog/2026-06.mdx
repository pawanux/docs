<Update label="June 30, 2026" description="Release 2026.06.2" tags={["Chatbots", "Voice AI", "Automations"]} rss={{ title: "Facebook Messenger for chatbots, emotion and unanswered-query detection, Scheduler" }}>

## Deploy chatbots to Facebook Messenger

Connect a Facebook Page and your chatbot will reply to Messenger chats on that page. Customers get the same answers on Messenger as on your website or WhatsApp.

## Emotion and unanswered-query detection

Two new per-agent settings help you see where conversations go wrong. **Emotion detection** picks up the customer's emotional state during the call. **Unanswered queries** captures the questions the customer asked that the agent couldn't answer. Use them to fill knowledge gaps and spot frustrated callers.

## Scheduler

Schedule recurring work with a visual cron builder. Pick how often something should run without writing cron syntax by hand.

### Voice AI

**Human transfer**: A human agent can now be chosen as a transfer target, so calls can be handed to a person when needed.

**Scripts from URL or text**: Load call scripts from a URL or paste them in as text.

### Evaluations

**Insights view**: A new view summarises insights across evaluated calls.

</Update>

<Update label="June 15, 2026" description="Release 2026.06.1" tags={["Apps & Data", "Billing", "Telephony"]} rss={{ title: "New form editor, appointments in Apps, consumption reports" }}>

## New form editor in Apps

Apps has a rebuilt form editor for designing the forms used to create and edit records.

## Consumption reports

Consumption reports break usage down by workspace and by agent, including the workspaces you own. See which teams and agents use the most credits and plan budgets around them.

## Appointments in Apps

Apps now have an **Appointments** tab for viewing appointments alongside your records. It appears whenever a calendar is enabled.

### Telephony

**Vobiz numbers**: Vobiz is available as a phone number provider.

### Apps

**Custom filters**: Save custom filters on app tabs and filter records by date range.

### Billing

**Refer & Earn**: Earn rewards by referring other companies to UnleashX.

</Update>
