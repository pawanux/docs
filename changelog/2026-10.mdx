<Update label="October 31, 2026" description="Release 2026.10.2" tags={["Billing", "Integrations"]} rss={{ title: "Recurring plan billing, promo codes, new WhatsApp partners" }}>

## Recurring billing for plans

Every plan screen now has a recurring checkout. Once you subscribe, your plan renews automatically at the end of each billing period, so nobody has to buy it again by hand and agents keep running without a gap in service.

## Company-level promo codes

Promo codes now apply to your whole company. Enter a code from the billing page and it covers every workspace in the company. Removing a code asks you to confirm first, so it can't be removed by accident.

### Integrations

**New WhatsApp partners**: Cunnekt and Watticks are now available as WhatsApp Business partners. That means more ways to connect a WhatsApp number to your chatbots and agents.

</Update>

<Update label="October 15, 2026" description="Release 2026.10.1" tags={["Automations", "Chatbots", "Voice AI", "Campaigns"]} rss={{ title: "Workflows become Automations, Slack deployment, missed calls" }}>

## Workflows are now Automations

*Workflows* is now called **Automations** throughout the app, to better describe what they do: run multi-step processes across your tools without anyone stepping in.

<Info>
  Only the name changed. Your existing automations, triggers, and run history are unaffected, and old links still open the right page.
</Info>

## Deploy chatbots and AI assistants to Slack

Slack is now a deployment channel for chatbots and AI assistants. A guided setup in the **Deploy** step connects your Slack bot, and your team can then talk to the assistant in Slack channels and direct messages, with the same knowledge and tools it uses everywhere else. See [Slack bot](/mcp/slack-bot).

## Missed calls

Voice AI has a new **Missed Calls** tab that lists inbound and outbound calls that didn't connect. Export missed calls for any date range, and the file is emailed to you when it's ready. Use it to plan callbacks or spot coverage gaps.

### Plans and billing

**Free and Enterprise plans**: Free and Enterprise plans are now available alongside the existing plans.

### Campaigns

**Conversation export**: Export all conversations from a campaign for offline analysis or reporting.

</Update>
