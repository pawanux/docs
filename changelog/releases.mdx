UnleashX ships continuously. Customer-facing changes are grouped into **two releases a month**, each labelled with its date and a calendar version: `2026.11.2` is the second release of November 2026.

This page lists the highlights of every release. For full release notes, open a month in the sidebar.

<Accordion title="How to read this changelog" icon="book-open">
  **Browse by month.** Each month in the sidebar has full release notes. On a month page, use the tags in the right-hand panel to filter by product area.

  **What's included:** new features, new integrations and providers, renamed or moved settings, and changes to plans and billing.

  **Each release** opens with its headline features, explained in a short paragraph. Smaller additions follow, grouped by product area.

  **Callouts:** an **Info** box marks a rename or a setting that moved. A **Warning** box marks a change that needs action from you.
