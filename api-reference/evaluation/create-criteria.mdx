---
title: Create Evaluation Criteria
openapi: "api-reference/openapi.json POST /evaluation/criteria/create-update/"
---

Create a criteria mapped to an evaluation config. `eval_id` is required.

Updating an existing one: see [Update Evaluation Criteria](/api-reference/evaluation/update-criteria).

## Scoring fields

Each call gets a score from 0 to 100 for this criteria. The thresholds decide the result:

| Score | Result |
|-------|--------|
| `pass_above` or more | Pass |
| Between `fail_below` and `pass_above` | Review |
| Below `fail_below` | Fail |

With the UI defaults (`pass_above` 70, `fail_below` 40) a score of 85 passes, 55 is "Review" and 30 fails. `review_below` is the top of the Review band, so it should be `pass_above - 1` (69 here). The UI sets it for you, and you can leave it out.

| Field | Meaning | UI default |
|-------|---------|------------|
| `accuracy_percentage` | Weight of this criteria in the overall score. It is relative to the other criteria of the same evaluation (weights 10, 10, 20 give 25%, 25%, 50%). Minimum 1. | 10 |
| `pass_above` | Pass at or above this score (0-100) | 70 |
| `fail_below` | Fail below this score (0-100) | 40 |
| `review_below` | Top of the Review band, normally `pass_above - 1` | 69 |
| `scoring_rubric` | Optional text that explains how to score. Saved with the criteria; the UI does not set it. | none |

Keep `fail_below` lower than `pass_above`.

## Criteria

Pick `criteria` from this list (send the name exactly). If you do not send `evaluation_prompt` when creating, the default prompt below is used.

| Criteria | Default prompt |
|----------|----------------|
| `Empathy` | Evaluate the agent's ability to recognize and validate customer emotions while maintaining professionalism. Score based on authentic acknowledgment of concerns and compassionate communication. |
| `Language Switch` | Assess the agent's linguistic proficiency in seamlessly transitioning between English and Hindi. Rate fluency, grammatical accuracy, and cultural appropriateness throughout the conversation. |
| `Turn Taking` | Analyze conversation balance by measuring speaking time distribution and interruption patterns. Score higher when both participants have equitable opportunities to communicate. |
| `Context Awareness` | Evaluate whether the agent references relevant customer history and prior interactions. Penalize redundant information requests and score based on personalized, informed responses. |
| `Recovery From Errors` | Assess the agent's response to mistakes through accountability, explanation, and remediation. Score based on professional composure and customer confidence restoration. |
| `Intent Recognition` | Evaluate the agent's ability to identify the underlying customer need beyond surface requests. Score based on clarifying questions and accurate problem diagnosis. |
| `Greeting Accuracy` | Assess the professionalism and completeness of the call opening, including identity verification and call objective clarity. Score based on adherence to service standards. |
| `Intent Confirmation` | Evaluate the agent's use of summarization and clarification techniques before proceeding. Score based on explicit customer acknowledgment of understanding. |
| `Probing Questions` | Assess the strategic depth and relevance of questioning. Score based on open-ended inquiry, follow-up rigor, and absence of leading or manipulative framing. |
| `Call Closing` | Evaluate closure completeness including action item recap, next-step clarity, and satisfaction verification. Score based on professional sign-off and unresolved issue identification. |
| `No Offensive Content` | Conduct thorough language review for profanity, disrespect, or cultural insensitivity. High scores require zero violations of professional communication standards. |
| `No Misrepresentation` | Verify factual accuracy, proper disclosure of limitations, and absence of exaggerated claims. Score based on compliance with truthfulness and transparency standards. |
| `Privacy Compliance` | Assess protection of personally identifiable information and adherence to regulatory standards. Score based on identity verification protocols and confidential information handling. |
| `Confidence Level` | Evaluate the agent's demonstration of product knowledge and solution competence. Score based on conviction in recommendations balanced with appropriate acknowledgment of limitations. |
| `Input Metadata Consistency` | Verify alignment between transcript content, call metadata, and recorded agent information. Score based on absence of contradictions across all documentation fields. |
