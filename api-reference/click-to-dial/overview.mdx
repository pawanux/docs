Click to Call bridges a call between your team member and a customer through your UnleashX number (DID). Trigger it from your own system with the APIs below, or from inside HubSpot or Zoho CRM. Every call is recorded and appears in **Voice AI › Click to Call › Call Logs**.

## APIs

| Endpoint | What it does |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/global/click-to-dial` | Trigger a call |
| `GET /api/v1/global/click-to-dial/history` | List call history |
| `GET /api/v1/global/c2c-live-status/{call_id}` | Live status of one call, or of today's calls when `call_id` is left out |

## Authentication

All Click to Call endpoints require a valid API token. See [Authentication](/api-reference/authentication) for how to generate and pass your token.

## CRM integrations

The HubSpot and Zoho CRM integrations need no code. An UnleashX admin sets them up in **Voice AI › Click to Call › CRM Integrations** and chooses which CRM users can call.
