# Microsoft Bookings

> **In one line:** Connect your Microsoft Bookings business so your UnleashX agent can check availability, book and reschedule appointments, and manage staff, services, and customers for you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Productivity & Scheduling |
| **Authentication** | OAuth (Microsoft 365 / Microsoft Graph) |
| **Setup time** | ~2 minutes |
| **Difficulty** | Moderate |
| **Best for** | Businesses that take appointments (salons, clinics, consultants, repair shops) who want an AI agent to handle booking, rescheduling, and availability checks |

## 1. Overview

Microsoft Bookings is Microsoft 365's online scheduling service for businesses. It holds one or more "booking businesses," each with its own staff, bookable services, customer list, and appointment calendar.

Once connected to UnleashX, your agent can look up a business's services and staff, check availability, create or reschedule appointments, cancel them, and manage the underlying staff/service/customer records — all in plain language. Instead of opening the Bookings page to add a new customer appointment, you can ask "book Priya in for a 30-minute consultation on Thursday at 2pm" and the agent finds an open slot and creates it.

<Warning>
Microsoft Bookings is a **shared, organization-level** feature — it only works with a **work or school Microsoft 365 account** on a plan that includes Bookings (e.g. Microsoft 365 Business Premium). It does **not** support personal Microsoft accounts (`@outlook.com`, `@hotmail.com`, `@live.com`) at all — not with reduced functionality, but not at all. If you try to connect with a personal account, authorization will fail outright.
</Warning>

## 2. What you'll need

- A **Microsoft 365 work or school account** on a plan that includes Bookings (Business Premium and most E-series plans qualify).
- A Bookings business already set up (or set one up at [outlook.office.com/bookings](https://outlook.office.com/bookings) after connecting — "Create booking page" under Shared booking pages, not the newer "Personal booking page" feature, which is a different, unrelated scheduling tool).
- An UnleashX account with access to **Data Connectors**.

<Note>
No API key required. Microsoft Bookings uses OAuth — you click **Authorize** and sign in with your work/school Microsoft account.
</Note>

## 3. Get your credentials

There's no key to copy. Authentication runs through Microsoft's secure OAuth sign-in.

<Steps>
<Step title="Start the connection in UnleashX">
Connecting Microsoft Bookings (Section 4) opens a Microsoft **Authorize** window.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in with your work or school Microsoft account">
Sign in at `https://login.microsoftonline.com` with the Microsoft 365 account that has access to the Bookings business you want to connect, and complete MFA if prompted. A personal Microsoft account will not work here — see the warning above.
</Step>
<Step title="Review and accept the permissions">
Microsoft displays exactly what UnleashX requests (see the scopes table). Click **Accept**.
</Step>
<Step title="Return to UnleashX">
You're redirected back automatically and the connection is complete.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Permissions (Microsoft Graph scopes) UnleashX requests:**

| Scope | Why UnleashX needs it |
|---|---|
| `Bookings.ReadWrite.All` | Read and manage the Bookings business itself, plus its staff, services, and customers. |
| `BookingsAppointment.ReadWrite.All` | Create, reschedule, and cancel appointments specifically. |
| `offline_access` | Keep the connection active by refreshing access without repeated sign-ins. |

<Note>
These are standard Microsoft Graph delegated scopes, enforced by Microsoft. None of them require tenant admin consent by default — any user with access to the Bookings business can connect it themselves. (Some organizations lock down *all* third-party app consent tenant-wide regardless of the specific permission; if your sign-in is blocked at the consent screen, that's an org-wide policy your Microsoft 365 admin controls, not something UnleashX can work around.)
</Note>

## 4. Connect on UnleashX

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your agent">
Go to [https://www.tryunleashx.com](https://www.tryunleashx.com) and open the agent you want to connect.
</Step>
<Step title="Open Data Connectors">
Inside the agent, go to **Data Connectors**.
</Step>
<Step title="Find Microsoft Bookings">
Locate **Microsoft Bookings** and click **Connect** (or **Add** / **Configure**).
</Step>
<Step title="Authorize with Microsoft">
Click **Authorize**, sign in with your work/school Microsoft account, and accept the permissions.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm success">
You return to UnleashX and the connector shows a **Connected** badge.
</Step>
</Steps>

{/* workflow-usage-section */}
### Use Microsoft Bookings in a Workflow

Once connected, you can add **Microsoft Bookings** to any automation from the **Workflows** builder. Its triggers and tools appear in the Apps panel, marked with an **MCP** badge.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a trigger node">
Open **Workflows → New Workflow**. On the canvas, click **+ Add Trigger**. In the **Paths** panel, open the **Apps** tab and select **Microsoft Bookings** — its **Triggers** are listed underneath. Use the search box if you have many connectors.
</Step>
<Step title="Add an action node">
Click the **+** below any node to add a step, then pick **Microsoft Bookings** again — this time the panel lists its **Actions**.
</Step>
<Step title="Configure the step">
Fill in the fields for the trigger or action you picked. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (*). Fields like **Business Id**, **Service Id**, and **Appointment Id** are searchable dropdowns populated live from your connected account — you don't need to type raw IDs by hand.
</Step>
<Step title="Add or select your account">
Under **Selected account**, choose an already-connected account, or click **Add Account** to connect one now.
</Step>
<Step title="Save and test">
Click **Save**. Use **Test** to verify the step, then toggle **Publish** when the workflow is ready.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The steps are the same for every connector. For the full workflow builder guide, see [Using MCP in Workflows](/mcp/workflows).
</Note>

## 5. Available tools

| Tool | What it does | Changes data? |
|---|---|---|
| List Businesses | Lists the Bookings businesses on your account. | No |
| Get Business | Retrieves details of a single business. | No |
| Create Business | Creates a new Bookings business. | ✏️ Yes |
| Publish Business | Makes a business's booking page public. | ✏️ Yes |
| Unpublish Business | Takes a business's booking page offline. | ✏️ Yes |
| List Staff | Lists staff members of a business. | No |
| Create Staff | Adds a staff member to a business. | ✏️ Yes |
| List Services | Lists bookable services offered by a business. | No |
| Create Service | Creates a new bookable service. | ✏️ Yes |
| List Customers | Lists customers of a business. | No |
| Create Customer | Creates a new customer record. | ✏️ Yes |
| List Appointments | Lists appointments on a business. | No |
| Get Appointment | Retrieves details of a single appointment. | No |
| Create Appointment | Books a new appointment for a customer. | ✏️ Yes |
| Update Appointment | Reschedules or reassigns an appointment. | ✏️ Yes |
| Cancel Appointment | Cancels an appointment and notifies the customer. | ⚠️ Yes |
| Delete Appointment | Deletes an appointment outright (no cancellation notice sent). | ⚠️ Yes |
| Get Calendar View | Lists appointments within a date range. | No |
| Get Staff Availability | Checks free/busy time for staff members. | No |
| On New Appointment *(trigger)* | Fires when a new appointment appears since the last check. | No |

## 6. Example usage

**"Book Priya for a 30-minute consultation this Thursday at 2pm."**
→ Runs Get Staff Availability or List Services to confirm the slot, then Create Appointment to book it.

**"What appointments do we have at the downtown clinic next week?"**
→ Runs Get Calendar View for that business across the date range.

**"Move the 3pm haircut to Friday morning instead."**
→ Runs List Appointments to find it, then Update Appointment to reschedule.

## 7. Permissions & data access

**UnleashX can:**
- Read and manage your Bookings businesses, staff, services, and customers.
- Create, reschedule, cancel, and delete appointments.
- Check staff availability and calendar views.

**UnleashX cannot:**
- Access your email, Outlook calendar, or other Microsoft 365 services — only Bookings data within the approved scopes.
- Connect using a personal Microsoft account — Bookings itself doesn't support it, regardless of UnleashX's configuration.
- Change your Microsoft password or account security settings.

**To disconnect:** In UnleashX, open **Data Connectors → Microsoft Bookings → Disconnect**. You can also revoke access at `https://account.microsoft.com/privacy/app-access` (or your org's My Apps portal for work accounts). Disconnecting revokes access immediately.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What it means | How to fix it |
|---|---|---|
| **"The scope ... does not exist" at sign-in** | You tried to authorize with a **personal** Microsoft account. Bookings' delegated permissions simply don't resolve for personal accounts — this isn't a temporary error. | Reconnect and sign in with a **work/school** Microsoft 365 account that has Bookings on its plan. |
| **401 Unauthorized** | Your Microsoft sign-in expired or the token is invalid. | Reconnect Microsoft Bookings in **Data Connectors** and sign in again. |
| **403 Forbidden / consent blocked** | Your organization's tenant-wide policy blocks third-party app consent, even though these specific scopes don't normally require admin approval. | Ask your Microsoft 365 admin to approve UnleashX, or adjust the org's user-consent settings. |
| **No businesses show up / "No Microsoft Bookings business found"** | The signed-in account's tenant has no Bookings business created yet. | Go to [outlook.office.com/bookings](https://outlook.office.com/bookings), create a booking page under **Shared booking pages**, then try again. |
| **Appointment action fails right after creating a new business** | A just-created Bookings business can take a short while to fully propagate on Microsoft's side. | Wait a few minutes and retry. |

For general MCP issues, see [/mcp/integrations](/mcp/integrations).

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**Can I use my personal Outlook/Hotmail account?**
No. Microsoft Bookings is a shared, organization-level feature and Microsoft does not support personal accounts for it at all — this is a Microsoft limitation, not an UnleashX one.

**Is my Bookings data stored by UnleashX?**
No. UnleashX reads and writes through Microsoft Graph live for each request; it doesn't keep a copy of your businesses, appointments, or customer data.

**Can multiple team members connect their own Bookings accounts?**
Yes. Each person authorizes their own Microsoft 365 account, so the agent works against each user's own accessible businesses.

**What happens if my Bookings business has more than one location/business?**
Most actions let you pick which business from a dropdown. If you only have one, you can leave the Business field empty and UnleashX will use it automatically.
